Self-care is often pictured as treats and time off. Those can help, but real self-care is the everyday care that keeps your mind and body steady.

## Everyday self-care

- **Basics first:** regular meals, water, sleep and movement.
- **Boundaries:** saying no to some things so you can say yes to what matters.
- **Rest that restores:** time that leaves you feeling recharged, not just distracted.
- **Support:** spending time with people who make you feel understood.

## Know your stress signals

Tension headaches, short temper, poor sleep and trouble concentrating are common signs that stress is building. Spotting them early lets you respond before they snowball.

If stress is affecting your health, work or relationships, talking with a mental health professional can help you find a plan that works for you.
