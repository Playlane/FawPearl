Everyone has difficult days and stressful seasons. It can be hard to know when what you are feeling is part of ordinary life and when it may help to talk with a professional.

## Common signs it may be time

- Low mood, worry or irritability that lasts for more than two weeks
- Changes in sleep, appetite or energy that you cannot explain
- Pulling away from friends, family or activities you used to enjoy
- Finding it hard to keep up with work, school or daily tasks
- Using alcohol or other substances to cope
- People close to you saying they are worried

## What getting help looks like

A first visit is a conversation. At Fawpearl, an Initial Psychiatric Evaluation gives you time to talk about what you are experiencing, your history and your goals. Together we agree on a plan, which may include therapy, medication or both.

You do not need to be in crisis to deserve support. Reaching out early often makes the path forward easier.

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text **988** or call **911** right away.
