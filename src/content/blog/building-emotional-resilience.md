Resilience is not about never feeling stressed. It is the ability to recover, adapt and keep going. The good news is that it can be built, one small habit at a time.

## Five habits that help

1. **Protect your sleep.** Keep a regular bedtime and wake time, even on weekends.
2. **Move your body.** A short walk can lift your mood and ease tension.
3. **Name what you feel.** Putting a feeling into words can make it more manageable.
4. **Stay connected.** A quick message or call to someone you trust matters more than you might think.
5. **Notice small wins.** At the end of the day, write down one thing that went well.

## When habits are not enough

If stress, worry or low mood keep getting in the way of daily life, it may be time for more support. Therapy and, when helpful, medication can make a real difference.
