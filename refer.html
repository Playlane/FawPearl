For providers

Refer a Patient

Psychiatry and therapy referrals for providers. Send us a referral and we'll reach out to your patient to schedule an online visit.

How referrals work

Fawpearl provides online psychiatric evaluations, medication management and talk therapy with a board-certified psychiatric nurse practitioner. We welcome referrals from primary care providers, therapists, hospitals, care facilities and community partners.

  1. 1
    Send the referral

    Request a call back below, or call us directly. We take the patient's details by phone.

  2. 2
    We contact your patient

    We reach out to schedule a video visit, usually within the week.

  3. 3
    We keep you in the loop

    With your patient's consent, we share updates and coordinate care with you.

Insurance

We're in-network with most major plans, including Anthem, Blue Cross Blue Shield, CareFirst, Cigna, Horizon, Oscar, Oxford, Quest Behavioral Health and UnitedHealthcare. Self-pay is also available. Fees & insurance

Our commitment

We treat every referral with care. If you have questions about a patient you referred, or about working with us, call +1 (240) 593-2260 or email info@fawpearl.org (please don't include patient details by email).

If your patient is in crisis, call 911, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or go to the nearest emergency room. Referrals are not an emergency service.
