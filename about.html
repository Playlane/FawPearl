About Fawpearl

Compassionate care, wherever you are

Client-centered virtual mental health care.

Our approach

Listening first, then building your plan

Fawpearl Health Services is led by Abimbola Shadare, a licensed, board-certified Psychiatric Nurse Practitioner. We provide compassionate, confidential and culturally competent care for depression, anxiety, trauma and other behavioral health needs.

Every plan starts with listening. We take time to understand your story and your goals, then build care that fits your life.

Our mission

To provide caring, accessible mental health support that helps people heal, grow and find hope again.

Our vision

A supportive community where everyone feels understood and no one faces mental health challenges alone.

Meet your provider

Abimbola Shadare

Licensed, Board-Certified Psychiatric Nurse Practitioner

Abimbola brings more than 8 years of nursing experience to every visit. She provides psychiatric evaluations, medication management, counseling and crisis intervention, using evidence-based, client-centered approaches.

Her care is compassionate, confidential and culturally competent, built around each person's story and goals.
